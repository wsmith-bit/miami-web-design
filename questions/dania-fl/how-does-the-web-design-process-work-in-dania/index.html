Step 1: Initial Consultation

Following the consultation, the web design agency, such as Miami Web Design, will create a detailed proposal outlining the project scope, timeline, and budget. Once the proposal is approved, the design and development phase begins. This step can last anywhere from 3 to 6 weeks, depending on the complexity of the website. Designers will create mockups and wireframes, presenting them to the client for approval. During the development phase, clients often need to gather or create content for the website. This includes text, images, and videos that effectively communicate their brand and services. Step 6: Launch

Once testing is complete and revisions are made, the website is officially launched. This is an exciting milestone for any business, marking the transition from concept to a live online presence. This stage typically takes a few days to finalize all settings and ensure everything is operational.
